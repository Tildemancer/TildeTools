# Chat shorthand and roleplay terms, TildeTools' own.
# The spellchecker accepts every spelling here in any case and never suggests one as a correction.
# Define shows these meanings first, before Wiktionary's, and an entry with none shows Wiktionary's alone.
# An entry: its spellings on one line, comma separated, then each meaning on its own line
# indented two spaces, as "part of speech: meaning".
#
# Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, wrote the meanings in this file and the notes marked Claude.
# These are the words a spellchecker gets wrong in FFXIV chat. They aren't misspellings, just
# words no dictionary expects: roleplay shorthand, game jargon, chat abbreviations, a few sounds,
# and some rare words writers love. They're never offered as corrections, because a typo should
# never be "fixed" into "lmao". ABOUT-CLAUDE.txt beside this file says where the rest of the
# lexicon comes from.

# Roleplay
# Claude: IC and OOC matter most here. Left out, they'd be underlined in nearly every RP line.

RP, rp
  abbreviation: Roleplay.
  verb: To roleplay.
RPer, rper, RPers, rpers
  noun: A roleplayer.
RPing, rping, RPed, rped
  verb: Roleplaying, roleplayed.
roleplaying, roleplayed, roleplayer, roleplayers
  verb: Taking part in a roleplay, playing a character.
  noun: Someone who roleplays.
IC, ic
  abbreviation: In character: said or done as your character, inside the story.
OOC, ooc
  abbreviation: Out of character: said or done as yourself, outside the story.
ERP, erp
  abbreviation: Erotic roleplay.
NPC, npc, NPCs, npcs
  abbreviation: Non-player character: a character run by the game, not a player.
PC, pc, PCs, pcs
  abbreviation: Player character: a character run by a player.
LFRP, lfrp
  abbreviation: Looking for roleplay.
godmodding, godmod, godmode
  noun: Controlling another player's character, or making your own impossible to affect, without their agreement.
metagaming, metagame
  noun: Using what you know out of character to act in character.

# Games
# Claude: the all-capitals ones, MSQ, PF, FC, LS and DC, have to be listed here. The game's own
# text never teaches them, because the game-word reader skips anything without a lowercase letter.

DPS, dps
  abbreviation: Damage per second. A measurement of damage dealt per second by an individual.
  noun: A type of class or job specialized in dealing damage.
AoE, aoe, AOE
  abbreviation: Area of effect: an attack or spell that hits everything in an area.
HP, hp
  abbreviation: Hit points: how much damage a character can take.
MP, mp
  abbreviation: Magic points: what a character spends to cast spells.
GCD, gcd
  abbreviation: Global cooldown: the shared wait between most actions.
CD, cd
  abbreviation: Cooldown: the wait before an ability can be used again.
ilvl, iLvl
  abbreviation: Item level: how strong a piece of gear is.
MMO, mmo, MMORPG, mmorpg
  abbreviation: Massively multiplayer online (role-playing) game.
GM, gm
  abbreviation: Game master: someone running the game or the story.
  abbreviation: Good morning.
DM, dm
  abbreviation: Direct message: a private message.
  abbreviation: Dungeon master: someone running a tabletop game.
PM, pm
  abbreviation: Private message.
MSQ, msq
  abbreviation: Main scenario quest: FFXIV's main story.
PF, pf
  abbreviation: Party Finder.
FC, fc
  abbreviation: Free Company: FFXIV's guilds.
LS, ls, CWLS, cwls
  abbreviation: Linkshell, cross-world linkshell: FFXIV's chat channels.
LB, lb
  abbreviation: Limit break.
DC, dc
  abbreviation: Data Center: a group of FFXIV worlds.
  verb: To disconnect.
GG, gg, GGs, ggs
  abbreviation: Good game.
ez
  adjective: Easy.
